Why Clank is special

Clank wasn’t made in a factory. He was built by hand, piece by magnetic piece, with care and creativity. Every click of a magnet was a choice, and every accessory he wears is handmade too. That’s what makes him one of a kind.

Clank’s Story

Clank has grown and changed over time, and every version is part of who he is. Here are his three builds.

Build 1

The X Pieces

It all began with a collection of X-shaped magnetic pieces. They weren’t a robot yet, but they were the very first beginning.

Build 2

The Cube

Next came a cube-shaped robot: a sturdy little block with a personality all its own.

Build 3

Clank

Then came Clank, his current form. He carries a bit of every build before him.
